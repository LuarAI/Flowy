# Topic notes (the raw material — replace with your own)

Unordered notes, quotes, and half-thoughts about the topic. The `angles`
node mines this file; entries may only claim what is supported here.

- Side projects compress the learn–build–feedback loop from months to days.
- The graveyard of unfinished projects is not waste: each one usually died
  *after* teaching its one lesson (deploys, auth, pricing pages, cold email).
- Finishing is a separate skill from building, and it is trained separately.
- Deadlines borrowed from real life (a friend's wedding site, a local
  club's signup form) beat self-imposed ones.
- "Learning in public" works less because of the audience and more because
  writing forces the learner to find the edge of what they actually know.
- Tutorials optimize for following; projects optimize for retrieving. You
  remember what you had to retrieve under mild panic.
- The second project in any domain takes about a tenth of the time of the
  first. Almost nobody starts a second one.
- Copying a thing that already exists (a clone) removes design decisions
  and isolates the learning to the mechanics.
