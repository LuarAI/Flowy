# Voice (placeholder — replace with your own)

Read by every writing node. The workflow gets better by making this file
better, not by making prompts longer.

- Honest and specific. Concrete examples over abstractions; numbers when
  they exist in the notes.
- One idea per entry. If a second idea shows up, it is a second entry.
- Confident, plain sentences. No "in this article we will", no summary
  paragraph at the end, no exclamation marks.
- 400–700 words per entry.
- It is fine to leave a question open. It is not fine to pad.

## Entry format

```markdown
---
title: ...
description: one sentence, ≤ 160 characters
slug: ...
---
```
